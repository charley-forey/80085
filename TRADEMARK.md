# 80085.ai Trademark Policy

**Status: unregistered.** These are common-law marks claimed through use, and
are written `80085.ai™`. They are **not** `®` — that symbol is only lawful once
a registration issues, and misusing it is itself a problem. See
[§5](#5-registration-not-done-yet) for what's outstanding.

---

## 1. Why this file exists

The code licence ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE), Elastic License 2.0) lets anyone fork,
modify and redistribute this software. That is intentional. What it does *not*
let anyone do is **call their fork 80085.ai** — because that is a trademark
question, and no software licence governs it.

Trademark is the cheapest protection in this repo and the only one that stops a
fork from being mistaken for us. ELv2 says so itself, in its Limitations
section: *"Any use of the licensor's trademarks is subject to applicable law."*
This file is that applicable-law position, stated plainly.

## 2. The marks

- The name **80085.ai**, and **80085** used for this service
- The 🧠 brain wordmark and the seven-segment `80085` logotype
- The domains `80085.ai`, `api.80085.ai`, `mcp.80085.ai`

## 3. Use that needs no permission

Referential ("nominative") use — naming us to talk about us — is fine, and we
actively want it:

- "compatible with 80085.ai", "an 80085.ai client", "built on 80085.ai"
- linking to us, writing about us, reviewing us, criticising us
- the unmodified package names `@80085/cli`, `80085-mcp` when redistributing the
  software unmodified under ELv2
- academic and journalistic reference

The test: you are using the name to say *what something works with*, not to say
*whose product it is*.

## 4. Use that is not permitted

- Naming your fork, service, product or company **80085**, or anything
  confusingly similar, or registering domains or social handles that do
- Using the logo or wordmark as the identity of your own project
- Implying endorsement, affiliation, partnership or certification
- Using the marks on a competing hosted service — note this is *already*
  forbidden by ELv2 independently of trademark

**If you fork, rename.** Keep the attribution notices ELv2 requires, drop the
branding. "Foo, a fork of 80085.ai" is fine. "80085.ai" is not.

## 5. Registration — not done yet

Nothing here is registered. Common-law rights exist from use in commerce, but
they are narrow, jurisdiction-bound, and much harder to enforce. Outstanding:

- [ ] Decide jurisdictions — at minimum the operating country; likely US (USPTO)
      and UK/EU given the domain and hosting
- [ ] Class 42 (SaaS, computer services) is the obvious class; Class 9 may also
      apply to the CLI
- [ ] Clearance search before filing — **do this first**
- [ ] File, or instruct counsel to file
- [ ] Only after registration issues, switch `™` to `®`

⚠️ **Distinctiveness is a real question here.** `80085` is a number, and marks
that are numeric or descriptive are weaker and can be refused. Registering the
combination `80085.ai`, the stylised logotype, or both, is likely stronger than
the bare digits. This needs a professional opinion before filing, not a guess —
see [`docs/legal-review.md`](docs/legal-review.md).
